About this view & date alternatives

Reading the chart

Each thin column is one verse, from Matthew to Revelation. Newer years are above older years. Color deepens as up to five distinct reported witnesses accumulate. Both date scenarios use the same year scale.

Optimistic uses the lower endpoint; pessimistic uses the upper endpoint of each complete reported date interval. These dates describe manuscript estimates, not the composition of the text.

Any reported surviving portion counts once. Contested witness/verse pairs are deferred and excluded from presence counts. Unknowns and missing entries do not establish absence.
